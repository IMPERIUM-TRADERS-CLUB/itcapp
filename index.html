 SUPPORT & DOCUMENTATION
Help Centre.
Quick answers for every part of the platform.

        

          
⠿
Bot Builder
Load a bot, change its blocks, set your stake, take profit, stop loss, martingale and number of runs, then press Run. Runs trade live on your selected account. Use the 💾 button to keep your bot in My Bots.

          
⚒
Bot Forge
Describe the bot you want, typed or spoken, for example “Over 1 with recovery Over 3”. The Forge builds the blocks, then you can test it, change it or save it. The badge shows Online AI or Basic mode.

          
◎
Analysis Tool
Watch live digit statistics. The green digit appears most, blue second most, red least and orange second least. Pick Over/Under, Even/Odd or Matches/Differs and a side, and your bot follows.

          
⌁
Charts
Live Volatility and Jump charts with ticks or candles. TradingView is there for forex, stocks and crypto. No orders are placed.

          
▤
Bulk Trader and Deriv Trader
Live digit pies, last results and chances for the trade you set. These pages are previews and do not buy contracts yet.

          
⚙
Account and safety
Log in with Deriv from Settings. Your password never reaches this site. Always test a bot with small stakes first, and never trade money you cannot afford to lose.
